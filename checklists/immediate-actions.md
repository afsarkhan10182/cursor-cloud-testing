- [ ] Outbound starts (week-1 product set only if seven is too many)

- [ ] Friday: first weekly review ([docs/07-weekly-review.md](../docs/07-weekly-review.md))

## Weeks 2–4

- [ ] Raise activity targets from the week-1 baseline
- [ ] Publish remaining long demos
- [ ] Put product page drafts live when ready (`website/`)
- [ ] Weekly review every week without skipping
- [ ] Every lost deal has a reason

## Day 30

- [ ] Fill product scorecard and conversion rates
- [ ] Decide: concentrate / two products / diagnose ([docs/08-month-end-decision.md](../docs/08-month-end-decision.md))
- [ ] Extend, adjust, or stop the salesperson pilot
