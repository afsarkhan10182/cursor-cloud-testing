# WhatsApp templates

Send after a call, or as a first touch if you only have a number. First touch should feel like one human, not a blast.

**Never** send 8 paragraphs. **Always** end with a demo CTA.

Replace [brackets]. Hindi mixed is fine if that is how the owner talks.

## After a call (confirm demo)

> Hi [Name], [Your name] from Digital Fuzed.  
> As discussed, I’ll show you how [Business] can manage [2–3 jobs] on the live software.  
> **Demo: [Day], [Time]**  
> WhatsApp this number if you need to shift.  
> Booked / any change — reply YES.

## First touch (no prior call)

> Hi [Name], I work with Digital Fuzed. We built software specifically for [salons / restaurants / schools / …], not a generic ERP.  
> If [Business] still runs [diary / KOT paper / fee Excel / WhatsApp leads], I can show the live system in 15 minutes.  
> **Book a free demo / ask for pricing** — reply DEMO or call +91 8459148396.

## After “send details”

> One-pager attached.  
> Live software is clearer than the PDF. Two slots: [A] or [B]. Which one?

## Follow-up 1 (day 3, no reply)

> Hi [Name], circling back once. If now is a bad month, tell me when to check in. If never, also fine — I won’t keep pinging.

## Follow-up 2 (after demo, no decision)

> Hi [Name], thanks again for the time yesterday.  
> Recap: you wanted [requirement]. Quote: [amount] [monthly/annual] + GST.  
> Happy to adjust scope, not silent-discount. Shall I send the payment / agreement, or is something blocking?

## Follow-up later (they said next quarter)

> Hi [Name], you had asked me to come back in [month]. Still relevant for [Business]? I can do a fresh 15-min demo if the team changed.

## Status / community (not spam groups)

> How a [salon] can manage appointments, billing, staff and inventory using Digital Fuzed.  
> Free demo: +91 8459148396

## Rules

- One product per thread. Do not list all seven products.
- No fake “sir your listing is incomplete on Google” tricks.
- If they say stop, Lost + NORESPONSE or INTEREST, and stop.
