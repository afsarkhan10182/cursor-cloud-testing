# Call script (spine, not a teleprompter)

Goal of the first call: **a conversation and a demo slot**, not a feature dump.

Length: 2–4 minutes if they are busy; 8 minutes if they engage.

## Before you dial

Know: business name, product you are offering, one likely pain, city.

## Opening (10 seconds)

> Namaste, am I speaking with [Name] — the owner / manager of [Business]?  
> This is [Your name] from Digital Fuzed. I’ll be brief. We make software that [one sentence for this product]. Is this a bad time for 60 seconds?

If bad time: *When should I call back?* Put that date in the tracker. Do not pitch over them.

## Permission + problem

> I’ll ask one question. How do you currently manage [appointments / orders / fees / stock / leads]?

Shut up. Let them talk.

## Bridge

If they describe a mess:

> That is exactly what we set up for [salons / restaurants / schools]. I can show you the live software in 15 minutes — your workflow, not a slideshow. When is better, tomorrow 11:30 or evening 6:30?

If they already use a competitor:

> Fair. Most people only switch if something is painful. What do you wish [current tool] did better?

If nothing: thank them, mark COMPETITOR or INTEREST, ask if you may send a 1-line WhatsApp in case it changes.

## If they say “send details”

> I will. Details without a demo are just a PDF. I’ll WhatsApp a one-pager **and** two time slots. You can decline after 15 minutes if it is irrelevant.

## If they ask price first

> It depends on monthly vs your own server, and on size. Typical range is [floor] to [ceiling] per month plus GST. I’ll quote a real number after I see your setup. Shall we book the demo?

Do not invent a discount on the call.

## Close the call

Always leave with **one** of:

- Demo date/time (calendar + WhatsApp confirm)
- Follow-up date they agreed
- Lost reason
