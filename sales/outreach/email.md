# Email templates

Use email for schools, hospitals, and anyone who asked for it. Many owners will still reply on WhatsApp — that is fine. Copy the thread into the tracker.

Subject lines: specific, not “Synergy / Digital Transformation.”

**Subject:** [School / Restaurant] software demo — [Business name]

Hello [Name],

Typical problems we replace: [two pains from the one-pager].

This is a product demo, not a custom-project meeting. Pricing is quoted after we see your workflow — monthly, annual, or your own server.

**Book a free demo / contact us for pricing**
