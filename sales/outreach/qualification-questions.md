# Qualification questions

Ask conversationally. You do not need all answers on call one.

## All products

1. What do you use today? (Nothing / Excel / WhatsApp / named software)
2. What annoys you about it?
3. Who decides to buy software — you, partner, trustee, IT, accountant?
4. Are you actively looking, or only curious?
5. Monthly subscription vs one-time vs your own server — any preference?
6. Timing: this month, this term, next year?
7. Budget range if they volunteer it — do not interrogate.

## Product extras

**Salon:** How do you take bookings? How many chairs/staff? Retail products?

**Restaurant:** Dine-in / delivery / both? How many tables? Current POS?

**School:** Students on roll? Fee frequency? Parent communication today?

**Hospital:** Beds / OPD volume? Pharmacy in-house? Insurance billing?

**Inventory:** SKUs roughly? One shop or more? GST invoicing need?

**Textile:** Trading / job work / manufacturing? How do you identify a lot?

**Real estate:** Broker / builder / both? Team size? Lead sources?

## Scoring (optional, in Notes)

- A: Pain + authority + timing this month
- B: Pain + authority + later
- C: Curious only
- D: Cannot buy

Only A and B should be booked as Qualified demos.
