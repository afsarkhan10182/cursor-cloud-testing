# Digital Fuzed — Salon Management

**For salon and spa owners who are tired of a diary, a WhatsApp chat, and a calculator.**

## The problem

Appointments clash. Staff commissions are guesswork. Product stock disappears. Walk-ins wait while you hunt for a number. You only know how the month went after someone types Excel on Sunday.

## The software

Digital Fuzed Salon keeps appointments, billing, staff, and inventory in one place — on phone or computer.

## What you can do

- Book and move appointments without double-booking
- Bill services and products in one invoice
- See staff performance and simple commission logic
- Track creams, colours, and retail stock
- Keep client history (last visit, preferred service, notes)
- Packages / memberships if you sell them

## How it is sold

Monthly or annual subscription. Setup + training if you need data moved. Own-server install if you insist (higher price).
