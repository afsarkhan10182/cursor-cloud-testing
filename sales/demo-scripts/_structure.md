# Demo structure (all products)

**Time:** 12–18 minutes. If they want more, they are qualified.

Founder joins if: custom workflow, hospital/school committee, or own-server IT questions.

## Before

- Confirm product + attendee + screen-share tool
- Demo account logged in, sample data loaded, names not embarrassing
- One-pager ready to WhatsApp after
- Know their current process from qualification notes

## Flow

1. **Reconfirm (2 min)** — “You said you struggle with X. If I only show that, is this meeting useful?”
2. **Happy path (8 min)** — one real job from start to finish. Do not tour every menu.
3. **Owner view (2 min)** — one report that answers “how is my business today?”
4. **Fit check (3 min)** — “What is missing for you to use this next week?”
5. **CTA (2 min)** — package options in words, not a 12-slide price deck. Book next step: proposal date or start date.

## Rules

- Click slowly. Narrate what a staff member would do at 7pm, not what a developer built.
- Never say “we can add that” unless it already exists or a founder said yes.
- Record with permission when possible.
- After: update tracker the same hour: Demo Completed, requirements, next date.

## CTA line

> Next step is a written quote for your size — monthly, annual, or own server. **Booked the free demo today; pricing follows in writing.**
