# Content / inbound (Track B)

Do not hire a content department.

Founders already understand the products. Record straightforward demonstrations of **actual software**, not generic ads.

## CTA (every video, same words)

**Book a free demo / Contact us for pricing**

Put WhatsApp +91 8459148396 on screen at the end. Link in description.

## Formats

- Full product demo (8–15 min, YouTube)
- 30–60 second feature demo (Reels / Shorts / LinkedIn)
- Problem → solution
- Before / after workflow
- How-to
- FAQ
- Feature spotlight
- Customer use case (only real)
- New feature
- Business-specific tip

## Reuse rule

One long YouTube demo becomes 5–10 short clips.

## Platforms

YouTube, Instagram / Reels, LinkedIn, website product page, WhatsApp status / communities where it is appropriate (no spam groups).

## Month-1 publishing cadence (founders have jobs)

Realistic, not heroic:

| Week | Long demo | Shorts | Notes |
| --- | ---: | ---: | --- |
| 1 | 1 (best demo-ready product) | 4 cuts | Also pin WhatsApp CTA |
| 2 | 1 (second product) | 4 cuts | Reply to every comment |
| 3 | 1 | 4 cuts | Repeat the message that got replies in outbound |
| 4 | 1 | 4 cuts | FAQ from real objections that week |

If time is short, **shorts from a screen recording** still beat silence.

## Title pattern

**How a [business] can manage [jobs] using Digital Fuzed**

Example: *How a salon can manage appointments, billing, staff and inventory using Digital Fuzed.*

## What not to do

- Paid ads before messages are proven
- Measuring success by views or followers
- Faceless slideshows with stock footage of handshakes
- Promising features that are not in the demo

A video with 500 views and 5 qualified leads beats 50,000 irrelevant views.

Track everything in `trackers/content-tracker.csv`.
