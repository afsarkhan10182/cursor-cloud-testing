# Strategy

## Current position

Digital Fuzed already has working software in several verticals. A small number of sales have happened. Sales are not yet systematic. Founders are doing this alongside regular jobs.

**The bottleneck is distribution, not more software.**

## Business model (do not lock yet)

Offer whatever the customer actually prefers, then record it:

- Monthly SaaS
- Annual SaaS
- Setup fee + recurring subscription
- One-time license / deployment
- Install on the customer's own domain or server (higher price)
- White-label / custom branding when the deal is large enough

The point of month one is to learn preference, not to enforce a model.

## Main rule

Do not build more products just because we can.

Use the existing products to find which market gives the strongest commercial opportunity.

## Do not pick the winner yet

We do not have enough evidence to say “salon is the main product” or “restaurant is the main product.”

Test several products. Measure:

1. Which receives responses?
2. Which receives demo requests?
3. Which gets serious conversations?
4. Which receives pricing requests?
5. Which converts?
6. Which has the shortest sales cycle?
7. Which customers accept recurring pricing?
8. Which requires the least customization and support?

After enough data, concentrate resources on winner(s). See [08-month-end-decision.md](08-month-end-decision.md).

## Team for the first month

### Founders

Product knowledge, technical questions, advanced demos, pricing, negotiation, closing, onboarding, product fixes that unblock sales.

### Hire #1 — one Sales / BD Executive

Find prospects → contact → qualify → book demo → follow up → help move the opportunity toward a sale.

Do not hire a sales team. Do not hire a marketing agency. Do not run paid ads until messaging is validated.

## Month-one goal

Not “maximum revenue in 30 days.”

Discover:

**Who buys, what they buy, why they buy, what stops them, and which acquisition channel works.**

## Core principle

First month gives the baseline. Months 2–3 tell us where to double down.
