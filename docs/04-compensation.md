# Compensation model (Hire #1)

Revenue is early. Do not start with a large fixed salary.

**Fixed monthly amount + performance incentive.**

Do not pay for raw leads. Pay for qualified opportunities and actual revenue.

## Recommended starting structure (founder must set the rupee amounts)

| Component | Purpose | Suggested starting point |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Fixed retainer | Makes the role real; covers research and follow-up | ₹12,000–18,000 / month for a 30-day pilot |
| Qualified demo incentive | Rewards real conversations, not spam | ₹250–500 per **qualified** demo completed |
| Sale incentive | Rewards closed business | 10% of first invoice **or** ₹2,500–7,500 per won deal, whichever policy you publish |
| Optional residual | Rewards recurring deals | 3% of collected subscription for months 2–6 on deals they originated |

Cap the qualified-demo incentive (example: max 20 demos / month payable) so the role cannot be gamed with junk demos.

## What counts as a qualified demo

Payable only if:

- Lead is in the tracker before the demo
- Qualification checklist passed ([03-pipeline-and-qualification.md](03-pipeline-and-qualification.md))
- Demo lasted a real working session (not “please just show the login page”)
- Decision-maker or influencer attended
- Founder can spot-check recordings / notes

A demo of an unqualified friend or a fake business does not pay.

## What counts as a sale

- Money received, or
- Signed agreement plus a dated payment commitment the founders accept

Discounts below floor price reduce or void the incentive (state this in the offer).

## What does not pay

- Uncontacted rows in the sheet
- “I sent 200 WhatsApps”
- Inbound leads the founders already had, unless the salesperson ran the process to close
- Custom project fishing that we decline

## Pilot terms

- 30 days, then review
- Either side can stop with 7 days notice during the pilot
- Leads and the tracker belong to Digital Fuzed
- WhatsApp business conversations must be exported / copied into the tracker weekly
- No authority to quote below the price floor or to promise custom features

## Offer letter bullets (copy into a real letter)

1. Role: Sales / Business Development Executive (pilot).
2. Retainer: ₹______ per month.
3. ₹______ per qualified completed demo, capped at ______ per month.
4. Sale incentive: ______.
5. Targets for month 1 are **learning targets**, not a firing metric: keep the pipeline clean, follow the workflow, show up to weekly review.
6. Confidentiality: product logins, prices, and customer data stay here.
