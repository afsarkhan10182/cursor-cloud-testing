# Job description — Sales / Business Development Executive

**Company:** Digital Fuzed  
**Location:** Mumbai (remote-friendly if they can call during business hours)  
**Type:** 30-day paid pilot, then extend if the process works  
**Reports to:** Founders

## The job in one line

Find business owners who might use our software, start a real conversation, book a demo, follow up, and help close.

## About us

Digital Fuzed already has working products for schools, hospitals, restaurants, real estate, inventory, salons, and textile businesses. We are not hiring you to “build a brand.” We are hiring you to talk to owners.

## What you will do every day

- Research local businesses (Google, Maps, LinkedIn, Instagram, directories)
- Add every prospect to our tracker (no leads only in your phone)
- Call, WhatsApp, email, and LinkedIn outreach
- Qualify: who they are, what they use today, what hurts, who decides
- Book product demos (you will be trained to run a basic demo)
- Follow up on a date, every time
- Help prepare simple quotes with founders
- Join a 30–45 minute weekly review

## What you will not do

- Buy ads
- Design creatives all day
- Promise custom software
- Discount below our floor
- Keep a private list of leads

## You are a fit if you

- Are comfortable speaking with business owners (Hindi + English)
- Can make cold calls without freezing
- Can use LinkedIn, WhatsApp, email, and Google Sheets
- Can research a business in 5 minutes
- Will use ChatGPT/AI to draft messages, then edit them so they sound human
- Are persistent with follow-ups without being rude
- Can learn to click through a software demo after training

## Nice to have

- 6–24 months of field sales, inside sales, or B2B calling
- Any CRM exposure
- Experience selling to shops, restaurants, schools, or clinics

## What we offer

- Fixed monthly retainer + incentive on qualified demos and closed sales (see compensation sheet)
- Direct access to founders (you will not be ignored)
- Real products to show, not a pitch deck only
- A 30-day pilot with a clear review

## Interview loop (founders)

1. Screen the voice note. If they cannot talk, stop.
2. 20-minute call: role-play a cold call (restaurant or salon).
3. Give them 10 businesses on Google Maps. Ask them to produce a 10-row sheet: name, phone, one-line hook. This is the work.
4. Explain compensation. Confirm they accept “no pay for junk leads.”
5. Paid 30-day pilot. One person only.
