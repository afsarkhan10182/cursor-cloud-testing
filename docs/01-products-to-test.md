| Textile | Job-work units, traders, small manufacturers | Large mill ERP replacements |

## Why each business would care (one sentence)

- **School:** Stop chasing fees and attendance on paper/Excel; parents see updates themselves.
- **Hospital:** Cut patient wait time, keep medicine stock visible, bill without chaos.
- **Restaurant:** Orders, KOT, tables, and payments in one place instead of shouting at the kitchen.
- **Real estate:** Stop losing leads in WhatsApp; track properties, site visits, and follow-ups.
- **Inventory:** Know what is in stock, what is selling, and what is about to run out.
- **Salon:** Appointments, billing, staff, and product stock without a diary and a calculator.
- **Textile:** Track orders, job work, stock, and billing across lots instead of registers.
